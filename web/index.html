Mathematical Formulation & Theoretical Foundations

Golden Rule of Mechanics · Pantograph Dynamics · Coulomb Friction · Symplectic Physics

1. The Golden Rule of Mechanics (Золотое правило механики)

In any ideal pantograph linkage with kinematic amplification factor $n$, the displacement gain at the suspended end is strictly coupled with force attenuation:

$$L = n \cdot H \quad \iff \quad \lambda = \frac{L}{H} = n$$ $$\Delta H = \frac{\Delta L}{n}$$ $$W_{\text{load}} = W_{\text{spring}} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad F_{\text{eff}} \cdot \Delta L = P_{\text{in}} \cdot \Delta H$$

• Rig A (uniform $n = 3$): $L = 3 \cdot H \quad (\lambda = 3.000\times)$
• Rig B (uniform $n = 5$): $L = 5 \cdot H \quad (\lambda = 5.000\times)$
• Rig C (scaled $1:2:3$): $L = (1 + 2 + 3) \cdot H_0 = 6 \cdot H_0 \quad (\lambda = 6.000\times)$

2. Linkage Force Statics & Pin Reactions (Баланс сил в стержнях)

At any scissor rhombus joint pin inclined at angle $\theta$ relative to the horizontal, supporting load $P_k$:

$$F_y = \frac{P_k}{2}$$ $$F_x = \frac{P_k}{2 \tan\theta}$$ $$R = \sqrt{F_x^2 + F_y^2} = \frac{P_k}{2 \sin\theta}$$ $$\vec{R}_{\text{upper}} = -\vec{R}_{\text{lower}} \quad \text{[Newton's 3rd Law]}$$

Note: At shallow scissor angles ($\theta \to 0$), lateral thrust $F_x$ surges asymptotically, multiplying pin compressive stress.

3. Coulomb Dry Friction & Stick-Slip Hysteresis (Сухое трение Кулона)

Joint pin dry friction introduces non-linear stiction and dissipative damping in the linkage:

$$f_k = \mu_k \cdot R \cdot (-\text{sign}(v_H)) \quad \text{[Kinetic Damping]}$$ $$|f_s| \le \mu_s \cdot R \quad \text{[Static Stiction Limit]}$$ $$\left| P_{\text{eff}} - \frac{k \cdot H}{\lambda} \right| \le f_s \cdot \lambda \quad \text{[Stick Deadband]}$$

Static stiction locks the mechanism inside an equilibrium deadband, creating discrete stick-slip steps on the $L(m)$ curve.

4. Stiffness Regression & Reflected Inertia (Регрессия и инерция $n^2 M$)

The suspended mass $M$ produces an equivalent reflected inertia amplified quadratically by the kinematic ratio:

$$M_{\text{eff}} = n^2 \cdot M$$ $$\frac{d^2 H}{dt^2} + 2\zeta\omega \frac{dH}{dt} + \omega^2 (H - H_{\text{target}}) = 0$$ $$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k_{\text{eff}}}{M_{\text{eff}}}} = \sqrt{\frac{k}{n^4 M}}$$ $$k = \frac{\lambda \cdot g}{\tan\alpha} \quad \text{[Experimental Slope Extraction]}$$
